A minor knight of the Rhineland, second son of a small house in Lindschied, church land of Mainz in the Taunus hills. A second son inherits nothing, so he took the cross and rode east with Barbarossa.

He lived through the emperor's death in the river and the long march to Acre, and somewhere on that road he stopped praying. God is the excuse; land is the goal. Now he carves a new Lindschied out of the desert, under a black rooster on gold.

His own people eat first: low taxes, full rations, churches and ale. They love him for it.

His enemies get nothing. No prisoners, no mercy for those who run. He burns the farms and lets hunger open the gates. He never shouts. He calls you "Kleiner". The calm is the threat.

The road east

From a village in the Taunus hills to the walls of Acre: some 3,500 km on foot and horseback with Barbarossa's host, 1189 to 1191.

  1. Lindschied, 1189. A second son inherits nothing. He takes the cross.
  2. Regensburg, May 1189. Barbarossa's host sets out down the Danube.
  3. Adrianople, winter 1189. The army waits out the winter in Byzantine land.
  4. Gallipoli, March 1190. Across the straits into Asia.
  5. Iconium, 18 May 1190. The Seljuk city falls to the crusaders.
  6. River Saleph, 10 June 1190. The emperor drowns. Most of the host turns home.
  7. Antioch, summer 1190. The survivors bury what is left of their emperor.
  8. Acre, 1191. The siege. Somewhere on that road, he stopped praying.

At a glance

Difficulty
Cold steel. He does not lose his temper, and rarely the battle.
Favourite troops
Swordsmen, crossbowmen and trebuchets.
Castles
Square stone fortresses, crossbows on every tower, pits before the gates. Eight castle layouts of his own.
Play style
Batters your walls with engines, burns your farms, then walks in with swordsmen. Kind to his own people, no quarter for anyone else.
In his voice
41 spoken lines in German-accented English, and 20 reaction videos: his big lines have their own, the rest pick from nine moods.
On the map

Add him to your game

Strong Hold (Mac)

  1. Main menu > Extras, then Get next to his name (Strong Hold 0.14.0 or newer). When he gets an update, the same place says Update.
  2. Or by hand: download the zip above (from the custom lords release), leave it zipped, then Extras > Import lord (.zip), or drop it on the game window.
  3. His face joins the lords in Skirmish > Add AI. The red (!) on it opens his page.

LAN games: every player needs the same version of him (Extras shows Update when yours is older).
